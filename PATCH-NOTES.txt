NahIDWin v0.7 source update

Changes
- MCM version bumped to 7; exactly three pages, two columns, three supplied images converted to 768x432 RGBA DDS.

- Included the supplied No staggercamera LE replacements (25 zero-byte HKX overrides) and configured the build workflow to package images and meshes into the MO2 install artifact.
